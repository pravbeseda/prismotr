# NNNN. Decision title as an imperative statement

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Source:** links to the documents or design notes it came from

## Context

What forced a decision. Constraints and facts, not intentions.

## Decision

What was decided, in one paragraph or a short list.

## Consequences

What is now mandatory, what became impossible, what has to be done next.

## Alternatives

What was considered and why it was rejected. An ADR without this section is useless.
